Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy covers information collected through jjosephcpa.com and through the course of a client engagement with J Joseph Enterprises, LLC.

What's collected

The contact form collects your name, email, phone number, and whatever you choose to share about your situation. If you become a client, tax preparation requires additional financial documents, which are exchanged through a secure link, never by email.

How it's used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare and file tax returns, and provide the planning and advisory services requested. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties except where required to prepare or file a return, such as with the IRS or a state tax agency.

How it's protected

Social Security numbers and bank details are never requested or accepted by email. Documents are exchanged through a secure link set up once a client relationship begins, and records are kept only as long as required by law or professional standards.

Questions

Email team@jjosephcpa.com with any questions about this policy or your information.
