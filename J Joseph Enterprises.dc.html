Services

Four services, one file

Most clients start with a return and stay for the planning. Each service stands on its own, and they share the same paperwork and the same accountant.

01

Personal and business taxes

Preparation and filing of federal and state returns for individuals, sole proprietors, partnerships, S corporations, and LLCs. Includes multi-state and self-employment income, rental property, K-1s, prior-year amendments, and correspondence with the taxing authority when a notice arrives.

Individual · Schedule C · Partnership · S-corp · Amendments

02

Tax planning

Work done before the year closes, when the numbers can still move: entity selection, reasonable compensation, quarterly estimates, depreciation and equipment timing, deduction and credit review, and projections for a year with a sale, a move, or a new business in it.

Projections · Estimates · Entity choice · Timing

03

Financial advisory services

A read on the whole picture: cash flow and budgeting, debt and financing decisions, bookkeeping clean-up, pricing and margin review for small businesses, and a straight answer on whether a purchase, a hire, or a loan makes sense this year.

Cash flow · Budgeting · Bookkeeping · Business review

04

Retirement planning

Choosing and funding the right plan — IRA, Roth, SEP, SIMPLE, or solo 401(k) — with the contribution and conversion math worked out against your bracket. For those closer to the date: withdrawal order, required distributions, and what each choice does to the tax bill.

IRA & Roth · SEP & SIMPLE · Solo 401(k) · Distributions
